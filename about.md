# Global MP3 Player

This mod completely replaces the background music in every level with a single custom MP3 file of your choice.

## Features
* Choose any `.mp3` file on your computer to replace the level music.
* Set a custom volume specifically for your replaced music.
* Configure a fade-in / fade-out duration for smoother transitions.
* Uses lightweight native FMODAudioEngine hooking for maximum stability.

## Why use this?
If you're streaming, recording, or just want to listen to your favorite song across all Geometry Dash levels without manually changing it, this mod handles it perfectly without any lag or audio glitches!
